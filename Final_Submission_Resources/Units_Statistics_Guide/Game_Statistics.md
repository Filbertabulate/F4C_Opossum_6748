# :crossed_swords: Unit, Enemy & Turret Stats: F4C Opossum

Quick-reference statistics for the units, enemies, turrets and abilities in the game. As there is no in-game tooltips, please keep this page open so that you are able to compare unit stats while playing.

> :bulb: **Tip:** Ranged units can attack from behind your frontline, while melee units must move into attack range. Keep this in mind when comparing units using their raw statistics.

---

## :coin: Player Units

Trained using **Gold**. Once deployed, units move towards the enemy base and attack automatically.

### Era 1: Undead

| Unit     |  HP | Move Speed | Damage | Cooldown |  DPS | Gold | Train Time |
| -------- | --: | ---------: | -----: | -------: | ---: | ---: | ---------: |
| Slime    | 110 |       1.45 |     12 |     1.1s | 10.9 |   50 |         1s |
| Orc      | 150 |       1.25 |     18 |     1.0s | 18.0 |   90 |         2s |
| Skeleton | 260 |       1.00 |     25 |    0.85s | 29.4 |  125 |         3s |

- **Slime** has the best HP-per-Gold in the era.
- **Skeleton** has the best DPS-per-Gold in the era.

### Era 2: Medieval

| Unit           |  HP | Move Speed | Damage | Cooldown |  DPS | Gold | Train Time |
| -------------- | --: | ---------: | -----: | -------: | ---: | ---: | ---------: |
| Swordman       | 200 |       1.15 |     26 |     1.0s | 26.0 |  120 |         2s |
| Player Archer  | 150 |       1.10 |     32 |     0.9s | 35.6 |  160 |         3s |
| Magic Swordman | 350 |       1.20 |     42 |     0.8s | 52.5 |  220 |         4s |

- **Player Archer** has the lowest HP of the three, but provides ranged damage.
- **Magic Swordman** has the highest DPS of the three.

## :japanese_ogre: Enemy Units

Defeating enemies rewards both **Gold** and **EXP**.

| Enemy             |  HP | Move Speed | Damage | Cooldown |  DPS | Gold | EXP | At Start? | Spawns Every |
| ----------------- | --: | ---------: | -----: | -------: | ---: | ---: | --: | :-------: | -----------: |
| Fire Mage         | 130 |       1.25 |     18 |     1.1s | 16.4 |   60 |  25 |    Yes    |          15s |
| Enemy Archer      | 100 |       1.15 |     20 |     0.9s | 22.2 |  150 |  30 |    Yes    |          20s |
| Golem             | 400 |       0.60 |     24 |     1.1s | 21.8 |  250 |  80 |     No    |          40s |
| Fire Giant (Boss) | 950 |       0.65 |     45 |     1.2s | 37.5 | 1000 | 200 |     No    |         200s |

- **Enemy Archer** has slightly higher DPS than the Golem, despite having much lower HP.
- **Golem** has the highest HP of any non-boss enemy.
- **Fire Mage** and **Enemy Archer** are present from the start, while the **Golem** and **Fire Giant** appear later.

## :european_castle: Turrets

Turrets automatically attack enemies within range. They can also be sold for **50% of their original Gold cost**.

### Era 1: Undead

| Turret         | Damage | Cooldown | Range |  DPS | Gold | Gold/DPS |
| -------------- | -----: | -------: | ----: | ---: | ---: | -------: |
| Basic Crossbow |     16 |     1.2s |     9 | 13.3 |  100 |      7.5 |
| Rapid Crossbow |     10 |     0.6s |     9 | 16.7 |  200 |     12.0 |

- **Basic Crossbow** is more Gold-efficient per DPS than the Rapid Crossbow, despite its slower attack rate.

### Era 2: Medieval

| Turret               | Damage | Cooldown | Range |  DPS | Gold | Gold/DPS |
| -------------------- | -----: | -------: | ----: | ---: | ---: | -------: |
| Heavy Crossbow       |     35 |     1.0s |    11 | 35.0 |  500 |     14.3 |
| Rapid Heavy Crossbow |     25 |     0.3s |    13 | 83.3 | 1000 |     12.0 |

- **Rapid Heavy Crossbow** provides higher DPS, greater range and better Gold-per-DPS efficiency than the Heavy Crossbow.

## :comet: Meteor Strike

Meteor Strike costs **EXP** instead of Gold and damages enemy units within its explosion radius.

> :warning: **Important:** Meteor Strike **cannot damage the enemy base**. Units must still be deployed to destroy the base and win the game.

| Statistic         | Era 1: Undead | Era 2: Medieval |
| ----------------- | ------------: | --------------: |
| EXP Cost          |           250 |             500 |
| Number of Meteors |            60 |             125 |
| Damage per Meteor |            50 |              50 |
| Explosion Radius  |           2.5 |             2.5 |

- Meteor Strike uses the same **EXP** required for evolution, so repeated use may delay progression to the next era.
